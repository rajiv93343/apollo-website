Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to common questions about our care, services, and facilities.

What services does Apollo Nursing Home offer?

We provide specialized orthopedic care, physiotherapy, diagnostic testing, general surgery, women’s health services, and 24/7 nursing support.

Do I need an appointment to see a doctor?

Yes, appointments are recommended to ensure timely consultation. Emergency cases are attended to immediately.

Is there an orthopedic specialist available at all times?

We have on-call orthopedic specialists available and regular visiting consultants for scheduled check-ups and surgeries.

Does the nursing home offer physiotherapy for elderly patients?

Yes, we offer tailored physiotherapy sessions focused on elderly mobility, joint care, and post-surgery rehabilitation.

Are X-ray and diagnostic services available on-site?

Yes, our Radiology & X-Ray Unit is fully equipped for quick and accurate imaging and diagnostics.

Can women consult for gynecology-related issues here?

Yes, we have doctors experienced in women’s health and minor gynecological procedures as part of our services.
